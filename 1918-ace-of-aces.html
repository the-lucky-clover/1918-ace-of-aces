94TH AERO · POCKET REFERENCE

Field
Almanac

Archie

British airmen’s nickname for anti-aircraft fire. Watch the gun’s tracking barrel, then change direction before the shell bursts.

Jerry

Allied slang for German forces. The campaign’s “Flying Circus” is Jagdgeschwader I, known for brightly painted fighters.

Mustard gas

A persistent chemical agent that contaminated ground and clothing. In combat, stay clear of yellow-green clouds; exposure damages the aircrew.

SPAD XIII

Single-seat French fighter: 220 hp Hispano-Suiza V-8, 135 mph top speed, and two synchronized .303-caliber Vickers guns with 400 rounds each.

Flying Circus

The mobile German fighter wing Jagdgeschwader I. Its squadrons moved by rail and used vivid aircraft colors for recognition.

The American Ace of Aces

A former race-car driver turned pursuit pilot repeatedly attacked alone or outnumbered. His 26 confirmed victories stood as the American record until World War II, and his courage earned the Medal of Honor. The standard he set is carried into every sortie.

Hat-in-the-Ring SPAD

SPAD XIII S4523 carried the 94th Aero Squadron’s Uncle Sam hat inside a red ring and period U.S. roundels. The player flies its spirit into an alternate-history arcade campaign.

Historical frame

The 94th’s records describe lone attacks, patrols against superior numbers, and a final tally of 26 victories. This campaign honors that record while letting the arcade score fly beyond it.

Historical background: National Veterans Memorial and Museum.

Aircraft specifications: National Museum of the U.S. Air Force · SPAD XIII C.1 fact sheet.

Drag to fly. Double-tap the combat screen for an invulnerable Immelmann. Guns fire automatically; desktop and gamepad pilots can bomb ground targets.

Each R icon is one emergency turn. Three are issued per level; spend them only when the bullet pattern closes.

Power

Shoot a drifting supply marker to cycle FIRE → WING → BLAST; its letter and color flip on impact. Collect only when the marker shows the power you want. The bright cockpit point is your hitbox.
