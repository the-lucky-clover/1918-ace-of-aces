94TH AERO · POCKET REFERENCE

Field
Almanac

Archie

British airmen’s nickname for anti-aircraft fire. Watch the gun’s tracking barrel, then change direction before the shell bursts.

Jerry

Allied slang for German forces. The campaign’s “Flying Circus” is Jagdgeschwader I, known for brightly painted fighters.

Mustard gas

A persistent chemical agent that contaminated ground and clothing. In combat, avoid yellow-green clouds or fit a Small Box Respirator.

SPAD XIII

A fast, rugged French fighter with a 220 hp Hispano-Suiza engine and twin synchronized Vickers guns.

Flying Circus

The mobile German fighter wing Jagdgeschwader I. Its squadrons moved by rail and used vivid aircraft colors for recognition.

Hat-in-the-Ring SPAD

Captain Eddie Rickenbacker’s SPAD XIII, serial S4523, carried the 94th Aero Squadron’s Uncle Sam hat inside a red ring and period U.S. roundels. The player flies that aircraft in an alternate-history arcade campaign.

Historical frame

Rickenbacker’s memoir records the 94th’s April patrol order under Captain Peterson with Lieutenants Reed Chambers and Rickenbacker, and October low patrols led by Chambers. His historical total was 26 victories; the score here intentionally escapes that ceiling.

Historical frame: Rickenbacker, Fighting the Flying Circus, Ch. 3 · October patrol account · victory record · S4523 airframe reference.
